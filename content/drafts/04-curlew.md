The UK holds a quarter of the world's breeding curlews. That makes their decline here a global problem, not just a local one.

Breeding numbers have fallen by more than half in 20 years, and in Ireland the curlew is now close to extinction as a breeding bird. The causes are well known: drained uplands, nest predation, new forestry on open ground and more intensive lowland farming.

If you've heard that long, rising call over a bog or a wet field, you'll know what we stand to lose.
