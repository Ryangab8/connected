European eels start life in the Sargasso Sea and travel thousands of miles to reach rivers here. Today, the number of young eels arriving in Europe's rivers is only about 1–7% of what it was in 1960–79. It's one of the most dramatic declines of any vertebrate in Europe.

The biggest problem is the way home. Weirs and dams block their return journey to the ocean. Pollution, illegal trade and shifts in Gulf Stream currents add to the pressure. The species is now Critically Endangered.
