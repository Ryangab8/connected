I compared how well five nations are delivering on their own commitments for nature. Northern Ireland came last.

On Connected Biodiversity's Policy Outlook, Northern Ireland scores 28 out of 100. To meet its own commitments it would need 70. That 42-point gap is the largest of the five nations I compared: Scotland, England, Wales, Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland.

Some of the reasons: Northern Ireland has no farm nature scheme on the scale of England's or Wales's, our rivers are among the most polluted in the UK, and Lough Neagh is suffering an algae crisis driven by agricultural run-off. Only 5.1% of land is protected, against a 30% target.

Scotland, by comparison, scores 78 with a 17-point gap.
