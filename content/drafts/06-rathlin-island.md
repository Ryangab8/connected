Rathlin Island, off the north Antrim coast, has pulled off a world first.

In March 2026 the LIFE Raft project, run by RSPB NI and the Rathlin community, completed the first ferret eradication ever achieved on an inhabited island. Rats are next. The brown rat phase is done and monitoring continues. If it holds, Rathlin will be the UK's first rodent-free inhabited island.

The seabirds are already responding. In 2025, Manx shearwaters bred on Rathlin for the first time in over 20 years. The island is home to more than 250,000 seabirds, including puffins, razorbills, guillemots and kittiwakes.
