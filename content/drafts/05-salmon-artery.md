Atlantic salmon don't just live in our rivers. For thousands of years they have fed them.

Salmon in Great Britain have declined by 63% over three generations and were classed as Endangered in 2023. Warming seas, river barriers, pollution and sea lice from fish farms all play a part.

Sediment records going back 2,000 years show salmon carried marine nitrogen and phosphorus upstream. When fewer return, that pipeline weakens. Otters switch to other prey, including native white-clawed crayfish that are already under pressure, and kingfishers lose some of the young salmon they feed on.
