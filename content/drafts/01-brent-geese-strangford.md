Every winter, a single sea lough just outside Belfast holds up to 80% of Canada's pale-bellied brent geese.

They arrive at Strangford Lough exhausted, after a non-stop flight via Greenland. That makes Strangford one of the most important waterfowl sites in Europe. It also makes the geese vulnerable: a population that depends on so few wintering sites has very little room for error.

The pressures sit at both ends of the journey. Here, it's the loss of the eelgrass beds they feed on. In the Arctic, it's a changing climate on their breeding grounds.
